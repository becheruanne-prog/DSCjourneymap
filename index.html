Zoom-in 2 · The Webinar Engine

The parallel front door · the highest-converting moment

Doctors who attend a Diana flagship webinar convert to Founding at 25-35% in the 48 hours after the event. No LP funnel comes close. The webinar is a 4-stage journey of its own.

Stage 1 · Capture · ~100%
Webinar registration page
Email + name + specialty + state + "your question for Diana" (the hook). Separate URL from /join/.
Stage 2 · Reminders · ~40% attend
3 triggered reminders
T-7 calendar invite · T-1 day-of · T-1h "starting soon" · If they asked a Q, Diana replies in 48h
Stage 3 · Live · 25-35% apply
In-webinar Founding offer
10-min in-webinar offer · pre-filled application opens · the single highest-conversion moment in the campaign
Stage 4 · Post · +5-15%
WF-07 Webinar Nurture

The math per Diana flagship event
250 registrants × 40% attendance = 100 live attendees × 30% conversion = 30 direct signups + 10 post-event recovery via WF-07 = ~40 Founding applications per event. Run 2 Diana flagships (Wk 6 + Wk 9 advertising) = ~80 signups · over 50% of the 150 target from one channel.
