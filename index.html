The two email buckets

Broadcast (capped) vs Triggered (unlimited)

Bucket 1 · Broadcast
Newsletter sends
Capped at 4 per month
Goes to every consenting contact on a fixed weekly schedule. Counts against the 4/month ceiling.
  • Week 1, 2, 3, 4 · Tuesday 10am ET
  • Same content for everyone
  • Used for: the monthly free guide, member spotlight, webinar invite, Founding offer CTA
  • One bucket, four slots, full stop
Bucket 2 · Triggered
Per-user behavior emails
Unlimited · per individual contact
Fires when one specific user takes one specific action. Does not count against the 4/month cap.
  • 3 nurture journeys (Fast Lane · Nurture · Webinar follow-up)
  • 17-touch Founding Lifecycle over 12 months
  • Layer 1 transactional (welcome, payment, profile updated)
  • Health-score alerts, save offers, win-back
The 4 monthly newsletter slots
