Part 2 · The compound behaviours, ledger view
Six patterns. Three new emails. One rule: ten contacts before you build.

Compound segments are higher signal than single-email reactions because they capture a pattern, not a moment. Each card below names the definition, what it means, and the treatment.

01
Multi-guide researcher

If: 2+ free guides downloaded within 30 days, no Founding application.

Studying DSC seriously but blocked — fear, financial, organisational readiness. Higher intent than single-guide downloaders.

Treat: Send executive-summary email. Do NOT enrol in Journey A urgency. Flow G.

Tier 1 · build Wk 6+ 1 email
02
Busy async-engaged

If: Registered, no-show, engaged with recording (clicked #16 link OR filled separate recording form).

Busiest doctors. Highest-LTV physicians. Wants content async — time-constrained, not low-intent.

Treat: Skip standard #16. Send async-path email with next flagship date. Flow F.

Tier 1 · build Wk 5+ 1 email
04
Fence-sitter

If: Opened all 4 Journey A emails, clicked ≥2 links, did not apply by Day 14.

Listening. Knows everything we're saying. Something specific is stopping them — and we don't know what.

Treat: Single Anne-signed plain-text email. Reply-only, no CTA. Flow H.

Tier 1 · build Wk 6+ 1 email
03
Eternal browser

If: 3+ webinar regs in 60d, ≥1 attendance, never applied.

Loves the content, won't commit. Real blocker (financial, regulatory) or competitor-observer. Needs human conversation.

Treat: Diana DM — NOT another automated send. Sales work, not marketing.

Tier 2 · vol-gatedno email
05
Research-then-convert

If: Downloaded a guide AND submitted Founding application within 14 days.

Best-case journey. They did their homework, then committed. Perfect testimonial source.

Treat: Modify Day 14 photo ask (#17) to ask "what guide convinced you?" — conditional on flag.

Tier 2 · vol-gatedmodify, not new
06
Founding ghost

If: Active member, score 0, no opens for 30+ days.

Paying-eligible member who's already gone. Will churn at Day 366. Detect by Day 14 of Founding journey.

Treat: Anne personal email Day 30 → Diana personal email Day 45. Extends existing Flow C.

Don't build all the segments. Pick by leverage.

Measurable lift, volume justified.

  • Multi-guide researcher — Flow G, Wk 6
  • Busy async-engaged — Flow F, Wk 5
  • Fence-sitter — Flow H, Wk 6
  • Founding ghost — extends Flow C, Wk 4
  • Webinar early-exit — uses #15 timestamp variant, Wk 5

  • Eternal browser — if 5+ regs by Wk 8

  • Cart-abandoner retargeting — if 10+ abandon by Wk 8

Part 6 · Critical pushback
Four real risks worth challenging.
01
Compound segments need volume to mean anything.
If only 4 contacts hit "multi-guide researcher" in 90 days, Flow G is a waste. Subject-line A/B on Journey B would lift conversion more. Decision rule: no compound-segment flow ships until 10+ trigger contacts.
02
Cadence-stacking risk.
A contact in Journey A who downloads a guide while flagged multi-guide-researcher AND fence-sitter could see 4 + 5 + 1 + 1 emails in 14 days. WF-00 Flow Router must enforce mutual exclusivity across Layer 3 segments.
03
Active-seeker detection needs a separate form.
To detect Compound 2 (#6) cleanly, the recording must be gated behind a discrete form, not the email link. Wk 4 website task. Difference between detecting cleanly vs. relying on link-click noise.
04
Retargeting doubles ad spend.
Six new audiences = six creatives, six audience sizes, parallel paid-social budget. Build audiences in 90-day window (pixel rules cost nothing). Activate ads only when budget exists.
