ii. The boundary — no email overlaps, ever Two consent records, two rail systems. Unsubscribing from one does not unsubscribe from the other.

BNM (in GHL)

Acquisition, nurture, content marketing, post-approval engagement

  • Cart recovery before payment
  • Source tracking, segmentation, content nurture

  • Member spotlight prompts (photo / video uploads)

trig · Day 300 of Founding lifecycle
3 emails over 30 days framing $87 vs $97 lock-in.
Critical boundary: the website team sends the renewal reminder at Day 358 and the renewal confirmation at Day 365. BNM stops upgrade nudges at Day 330 so the contact gets clean air before the transactional renewal flow. No overlap.
Phase 4
Re-engagement & hygiene
Standard GHL handling for quiet contacts. Owned by BNM because these sequences live in BNM's lists.
19
Engagement Score Updates A
trig · any open, click or event in BNM email
Increments engagement_score. Updates intent tag if threshold crossed.
20
Re-Engagement Sequence A
trig · 60 days, no opens / clicks
Reduces to monthly. At 90 days, one re-engagement email, then archive.
21
Newsletter-Only Downgrade A
trig · click "just send the newsletter"
Removes active sequence tag, keeps newsletter consent, moves to Branch N.
22
Unsubscribe Handler A
trig · click unsubscribe in any BNM email
Sets state:suppressed, removes all marketing consent, halts BNM sequences.
Two consent records. Unsubscribes on BNM emails do not cancel the website team's transactional emails. Payment-failure notices, renewal confirmations and password resets keep firing — legally required, operationally necessary.
Phase 5
Campaign-specific (time-limited)
Built once. Activated for a window. Deactivated when the window closes.
23
Final-Week Countdown B
trig · final 7 days before Founding close
Daily Mon→Sun countdown emails, urgency-driven. Deactivated after deadline.
24
Win-Back · Founding Non-Converters B
trig · Day 31 post-deadline (1 Aug)
6 emails over 30 days. Offers Alliance at standard rate. Exits on conversion or Day 30.
25
Member Referral Capture A
trig · /join/?ref={member_id}
Stamps referring member ID on new contact. Swaps in personalised welcome.
Note: these three are dormant by default. WF-23 lights up in the run-up to cohort close; WF-24 fires the day after; WF-25 is always-on but only activates when a referral link is used.

vi. Decisions to lock with the website team before launch

Five things that need a yes from the web team in writing.

If any one of these is wrong on launch day, two systems will message the same contact in the same week without either side knowing.
01 Confirm Cart Recovery (WF-09) is BNM-owned — the website team is not also sending recovery emails on Step 2 abandonment. Owner · WF-09
02 Confirm the website team's Founding Welcome fires before BNM's Onboarding Nurture — Day 3 start gives a clean 3-day buffer. Timing · WF-15
03 Confirm two consent records exist: BNM marketing + web transactional. Unsubscribing from one does not unsubscribe from the other. Consent · WF-22
04 Confirm webhook routing: Stripe → web backend → GHL, or Stripe → GHL directly. WF-06 tags and the WF-15 trigger depend on this. Webhooks · WF-06, 15
05 Confirm Member Upload Prompts (WF-17) are BNM territory — the website team is not also asking members for content uploads. Owner · WF-17
